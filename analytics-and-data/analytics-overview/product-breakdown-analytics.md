# Product Breakdown Analytics

Product Breakdown is a tabular view of how many products variants, orders, and associated revenue can be attributed per product variant. The product breakdown helps you answer questions such as "what product variant are people subscribing to the most?" and "what product variant is bringing in the most subscription revenue."&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

By leveraging the filters at the top of the table, you can view:&#x20;

* Order count, product units, or revenue, for first subscription orders by product variant&#x20;
* Order count, product units, or revenue, for all subscription orders by product variant&#x20;

The data can be viewed by day or by month.&#x20;
