# Cohort Analysis

Cohort analysis provides retention data around your subscriptions based on when an initial subscription was purchased. You can toggle between seeing the contract retention rate and the number of subscriptions. This retention curve is also plotted over time in a linear fashion. The flatter the curve, the better your retention.
