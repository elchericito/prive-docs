# Subscriptions Analytics

The Subscriptions tab of Recurly Commerce Analytics shows charts that demonstrate trends around the count of subscriptions over a period of time, compared to a previous period. The dotted line shows data for the previous period so you can easily compare what’s going on now, with what happened before.
