# ✨ Editing the Customer Portal

{% hint style="info" %}
The customer portal is where your subscribers go to manage/view their subscription contract. As a merchant, you can edit the permissions and functionality your subscriber have in their customer portal in addition to changing the style of the user interface. Navigate to the “Customer Portal” tab in the Recurly Commerceapp to make changes.
{% endhint %}

## Customer Portal Permissions

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (26).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Configure what permissions subscribers have access to in their portal by toggling on or off the following 7 switches:

1. **Shoppers can skip**: If enabled, customers will be able to skip their next subscription shipment. This will not cancel their subscription. Enabling skips allows flexibility for the customer in the event that they might have too much product, or are away for that delivery cycle.
2. **Shoppers can change renewal date:** If enabled, customers will be able to change when their subscription is delivered. Customers will be able to pick any date up until their next scheduled delivery.
3. **Shoppers can change quantity of products:** If enabled, customers will be able to update the quantity of their products. Any special discounts applied will automatically be updated.
4. **Shoppers can change delivery cadence:** If enabled, customers will be able to change how often their subscription is delivered. Customers will only be able to change the delivery cadence to other delivery cadence options that are tied to the subscription plan for that product. Allowing cadence changes helps customers correct how often they want a delivery to adapt to their lifestyle.
5. **Shoppers can swap products:** If enabled, customers will be able to swap the product tied to their subscription to something else. Customers will only be able to swap to other products that have the same subscription plan. Allowing product swaps enables customers to try something new without needing to cancel their subscriptions.
6. **Shoppers can pause and un-pause:** If enabled, customers will be able to pause their subscriptions. Allowing customers to pause provides flexibility when a customer temporarily does not need product. As a merchant, configure whether subscribers have an indefinite pause or if it lasts a specific number of deliveries.
7. **Shoppers can gift next upcoming delivery:** If enabled, customers will be able to send their upcoming delivery to a friend as a gift, instead of getting it delivered to themselves.&#x20;

### Stylize the Customer Portal

Stylize the portal login page and customer portal by selecting a template, choosing colors, changing borders, changing the text of the login portal page. Be sure to click the “save and set live” button to see all changes reflected. If you would like specific copy to be changed on buttons in the portal, please reach out to your CSM.&#x20;
