# 🚀 Automations

{% hint style="info" %}
To create one of the following automations, navigate to the “Automations” tab of the Recurly Commerceapp, and select the type of automation you would like to configure. This feature is available for merchants on the Recurly CommercePro and Enterprise plans.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

<table data-view="cards"><thead><tr><th></th><th></th><th></th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Swap automation</strong> </td><td>Use: Free Trial, Product Swap</td><td><ul><li>Enable your customers to try a product for free, then auto-upgrade your customers after the free trial window </li><li>Allow your customers to try new products every renewal by unlocking a product swap regimen automation </li></ul></td></tr><tr><td><strong>Add-on Automation</strong> </td><td>Use: Gifting, Loyalty </td><td><ul><li>Reward your subscribers by auto-adding gift SKUs at specific intervals in their subscription journey (i.e every 5th renewal) </li></ul></td></tr></tbody></table>
