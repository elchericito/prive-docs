# 📎 Understanding subscriber activity

{% hint style="info" %}
In each subscription, which can be found in both the “Subscribers" and “Subscriptions” tab of the Recurly Commerceapp, there is an order history and activity log at the very bottom of the contract that allows merchants to view and track all activity made on the contract.
{% endhint %}

### View Order History

In each subscription contract Recurly Commercetracks each successful order as individual line items and records the order number, date, subtotal, tax, shipping, and total price of the order processed.

Click the purple highlighted links to be re-directed to the Shopify orders page where you can make further actions like [refunds](https://www.notion.so/5f85bcf7af2c469ca204862f767d6aac?pvs=21) or [fulfillments](https://www.notion.so/7b97afae1e684196a33be1892c9944ec?pvs=21).

### Customer Activity Log

When answering a customer support ticket, merchants have full visibility into an account thanks to this log. There are 3 different intent signals for the actions made:

1. **Store Admin:** This is signal appears if the action/change was made by the merchant to the subscription contract through the Recurly Commerceapp.
2. **Customer:** This is signal appears if the action/change was made by the customer to their own subscription contract through their customer portal.
3. **Recurly CommerceSystem:** This is signal appears if the action/change was made by the Recurly Commerceteam or system to the subscription contract.
