# 🌠 Changing your Prive Plan

{% hint style="info" %}
In the Prive app under the “Settings” tab, you can view your current plan and upgrade at anytime to unlock advanced features, a dedicated CSM, shorter SLA response times and more.
{% endhint %}

To change your current plan, select the “change plan” button in the top right hand corner.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (10).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To learn more about our pricing [view our detailed plan comparison document here](https://www.tryprive.com/pricing).

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (23).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once the plan of choice has been selected, you will be re-directed to Shopify where you will approve the monthly charges and confirm your new subscription plan to Prive.

Return to the “Settings” tab of your Prive app to see your new plan reflected in your portal.
