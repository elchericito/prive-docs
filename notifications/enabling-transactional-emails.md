# 📪 Enabling transactional emails

{% hint style="info" %}
Recurly Commerce recommends toggling on 4 or more email notifications so your subscribers are always in the loop and customer support inbound is not a headache for your team.
{% endhint %}

The following 7 comms are available for email:

1. **Subscription created:** Confirms that a subscription order has been created
2. **Order placed:** Confirms that an order has been placed _(highly recommended)_
3. **Failed payment method:** Notifies customers when their order could not be placed due to payment failure _(highly recommended)_
4. **Subscription cancelled:** Confirms that a subscription contract has been cancelled
5. **Product out of stock:** Notifies customers when one or more products they are subscribed to are out of stock _(highly recommended)_
6. **Upcoming order:** Notifies customers 3 days before their subscription order is due to renew _(highly recommended)_
7. **Gift Subscription Created:** Confirms that a gift subscription order has been created
