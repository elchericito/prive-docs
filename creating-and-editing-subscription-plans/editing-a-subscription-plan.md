# ✏️ Editing a subscription plan

{% hint style="info" %}
To edit the subscription plan, navigate to the “subscription plans” tab, select the 3 gray dots next to the plan, and click edit.
{% endhint %}

### Archive a Subscription Plan/Option

In the top right hand corner of the subscription plan, select the 3 gray dots and click “archive” to deactivate a plan. View all archived plans in the “Archived” tab at the top of the page.

_**Note:** Archiving a live plan with active subscribers attached to it will not cancel the active subscription contracts. These subscribers will still renew. Please be mindful when archiving an active plan. Once a plan has been archived it cannot be reactivated._

### Add or Remove Products

To add or remove products from the subscription plan, scroll down to the section labeled “Details” and select the purple “edit” button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-12-13 at 1.00.17 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* You have the option to rename the subscription offer group name and the description, view existing products on the subscription plan, and add or remove existing products.
*   To add or remove existing products from the subscription plan, click the “+ select products” button and a list of your full Shopify store’s product SKUs will appear. (Use the search bar to filter for a specific product) Click the checkbox next to products you would like to add to the subscription plan and uncheck products you would like to remove.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-12-13 at 12.50.35 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-12-13 at 12.52.20 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

_**Note:** Be sure to select both the purple “confirm” and “save changes” buttons to have the changes reflected._

### Edit Purchase Options & Discounts

To edit the purchase options and frequency discounts attached, scroll down to the section labeled “Options” and select the purple “edit” button. In this section you will see each purchase option listed with the delivery cadence and # of subscribers with each frequency in their subscription contract.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-12-13 at 12.59.49 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*   Once you have selected edit, you can rank the order in which each subscription option appears on your subscription widget to your customers, archive existing subscription purchase options, or add new purchase options. You also can toggle on or off the [gifting feature](https://www.tryprive.com/blog/gifting) which will allows customers to gift a subscription to family member or friend.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-12-13 at 12.56.41 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Be sure to click “Save Changes” in the top right hand corner to have the changes reflected.

### Edit Product Swaps & Add-ons

To edit the products available for swaps and add-ons scroll to the section labeled “Product Swap and Add-ons” and select the purple “edit” button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-12-13 at 12.58.32 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* To view existing products on the subscription plan that are available for swaps or add-ons, remove or add products, click the “+ select products” button and a list of your full Shopify store’s product SKUs will appear. (Use the search bar to filter for a specific product)
*   Click the checkboxes to check or uncheck products you would like to be available for swaps, one time add-ons, and/or subscription add-ons.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-12-13 at 1.04.49 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

_**Note:** Be sure to select both the purple “confirm” and “save changes” buttons to have the changes reflected._

### Edit Special Discounts

To edit special discounts, scroll down to the section labeled “Special Discounts” and select the purple “edit” button. In this section you will see a special discount configured by order number or by quantity.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-12-13 at 12.58.13 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*   One the discount has been configured, you will be able to edit or the existing special discount or turn off the special discount by selecting the button labeled “None” at the top of the page. Be sure to select the purple button in the top right hand corner labeled “Save Changes” to insure all changes are reflected.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-12-13 at 1.05.45 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Edit Free Shipping

To edit free shipping, scroll down to the last section labeled “Free Shipping” and select the purple “edit” button. In this section you will see the current free shipping offer that is configured for your subscribers.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-12-13 at 12.58.53 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*   Toggle Free Shipping on or off, change the selected countries and set or remove minimum purchase requirements. Be sure to select the purple button in the top right hand corner labeled “Save Changes” to insure all changes are reflected.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screen Shot 2022-12-13 at 1.06.47 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
